What's in the box

  • The base, assembled: a round white 3D-printed base with 7 speed buttons and an on/off rocker
  • A UL-listed 12 V wall adapter and a 12 V LED bulb (replaceable)
  • 30 film units with their fold lines laser-scored: 25 standard units and 5 collar units that seat the shade in the base
  • A QR code to the folding guide, as a PDF and a video

The kit has the exact number of units. If one gets damaged, we'll send a replacement.

Details

ShadeTriangle-Edge Icosahedron, about 8 in (20 cm) across. Every face is three kite-shaped pieces
AssemblyFolded and locked by hand. No glue, clips or tape
Shade materialFrosted, translucent polyester (PET) film
BaseØ200 × 60 mm (7.9 × 2.4 in), white
RotationThe shade turns, the light stays still. 1, 2.5 or 5 turns a minute, either way, plus stop
Light12 V LED bulb, E12 base. Switched with the rocker
Power12 V wall adapter, included. Nothing in the lamp runs on mains power
Folding timeAs long as you like. It's a project, not a chore

Safety

  • Use only the 12 V adapter that comes with the lamp, and only a 12 V LED bulb of the supplied type. Never an incandescent or halogen bulb: the shade is plastic film
  • Indoor use only. Keep it dry, and don't cover the shade or put fabric or paper on it
  • Keep the turning base clear of hair, cords and small fingers. Not a toy: contains small parts

Origami credit

The shade is folded from the Triangle Edge Module, designed by Lewis Simon, in its half-square version by Bennett Arnstein, from 3-D Geometric Origami: Modular Polyhedra by Rona Gurkewitz and Bennett Arnstein (1995). We're grateful to the origami designers whose work makes this lamp possible.

How it goes together

An evening project, or several

Fold 30 units

Each unit starts as a half-square film sheet with its creases already scored. The guide shows every fold.

Build the ball

Join the units edge by edge, starting from the collar ring at the bottom. The locks hold by friction and folding alone.

Seat and switch on

The collar slots into the pocket on the base. Flip the rocker, press a speed button, and turn the lights down.
