How it works

Fold. Lock. Turn.

Fold the units

Every crease is laser-scored into the film, so each fold lands in the same place on all 30 units. Follow the PDF or the video, at your own pace.

Lock them together

The units slot into one another to form a solid icosahedron. Each face is three kite-shaped pieces. It holds by folding alone: no glue, clips or tape.

Set it turning

Drop the shade into the pocket on the base. The shade turns; the light stays still on its own post, so no wires ever twist.

The base

One base. Every shade.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need origami experience?

Some patience helps more than experience. The fold lines are laser-scored, and the guide shows every step as a PDF and a video. It's a real folding project, so take as long as you like.

Is there really no glue?

None. Every shade in the line has to lock by folding alone and stay together while it turns and when you lift it off the base. Designs that need glue, clips or tape don't make the cut.

What is the shade made of?

A frosted, translucent polyester (PET) film. It's tougher than paper and spreads the light into an even glow.

Is it safe to leave on?

The lamp is LED only and runs on a UL-listed 12 V wall adapter; nothing inside runs on mains power. Use only the supplied adapter and the 12 V LED bulb type it comes with, never an incandescent or halogen bulb.

What if I tear a unit?

The kit has exactly the units you need. If one gets damaged, contact us and we'll send a replacement.

Can I buy the lamp already folded?

Not for now. Drop 01 is a kit: folding the shade is the point.

Do you ship?

The first batch is for Los Angeles fairs and local pickup. Shipping within the US comes after that. People on the list hear first.
