How it works

Fold. Assemble. Glow.

Fold the units

Every crease is laser-perforated, so each fold lands clean and the same every time. No precision required.

Lock them together

Thirty identical modules slot into each other to form a kusudama: a geometric ball held together by friction, no glue.

Set it spinning

Seat the ball on the base. The light stays still while the shell turns slowly, throwing soft facets across the room.

FAQ

Questions

What is a drop?

A small, limited batch. When it's ready, the waitlist gets an email first with a short early-access window. Anything left goes on public sale afterwards.

Do I need origami experience?

No. The perforated fold lines do the hard part, and the guide walks through every step with illustrations.

Is it safe to leave on?

The lamp uses a low-heat LED only, never an incandescent bulb, so the film stays cool. It runs on a 12 V wall adapter.

What is the shell made of?

Duralar Matte, a frosted polyester film. It's tougher than paper, won't yellow, and diffuses light into an even glow.

Do you ship?

We're starting in Los Angeles at markets and will add shipping within the US. Waitlist members hear first.
